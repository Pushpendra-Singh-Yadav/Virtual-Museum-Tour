The National Museum, New Delhi

The premier museum of India, showcasing a treasure trove of art, culture, and heritage.

About the Museum

The National Museum, established in 1949, holds the honor of being India’s premier cultural institution. It was founded to collect, preserve, and display a diverse range of artifacts spanning over 5,000 years of Indian civilization.

Located in the heart of New Delhi, the museum houses a vast collection of more than 200,000 works of art, including artifacts, paintings, coins, manuscripts, and weaponry. The museum's galleries are meticulously curated to tell the story of India's rich history, from prehistoric times to the modern era, making it a crucial center for research and education in Indian history and arts.

A Glimpse into the Collections

Harappan Civilization Gallery

This gallery is home to artifacts from the Indus Valley Civilization (c. 2600–1900 BCE), one of the world's oldest urban cultures. Key exhibits include the iconic bronze 'Dancing Girl' sculpture, pottery, seals, and terracotta figurines that offer a fascinating look into the daily life and artistic achievements of the ancient civilization.

Buddhist Art Gallery

The museum’s Buddhist art collection is unparalleled. It features rare artifacts from the Mauryan, Sunga, and Kushana periods, including relics of the Buddha from Piprahwa. The gallery's centerpiece is a colossal statue of a standing Buddha from Mathura, carved in red sandstone.

Indian Miniature Painting Gallery

Explore the intricate world of Indian miniature paintings from various schools like Mughal, Rajasthani, and Pahari. These exquisite paintings, often made with natural pigments, depict mythological stories, royal portraits, and scenes from daily life with incredible detail and vibrancy.

Coin Collection

The Numismatics section is one of the richest in Asia, with coins spanning from ancient to medieval periods. The collection chronicles the history of Indian kingdoms and trade routes through their currency, including punch-marked coins, Gupta gold coins, and Sultanate-era silver coins.

Arms and Armour Gallery

This gallery showcases the evolution of military technology in India. It features a wide range of swords, daggers, bows, and other weapons used by ancient and medieval warriors. The craftsmanship is breathtaking, with many pieces adorned with intricate gold and silver inlay work.
