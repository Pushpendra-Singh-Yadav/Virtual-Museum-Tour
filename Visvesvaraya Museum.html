Visvesvaraya Industrial and Technological Museum

An interactive journey through the wonders of science and technology.

About the Museum

Inaugurated in 1962, the Visvesvaraya Industrial and Technological Museum (VITM) in Bengaluru is a constituent unit of the National Council of Science Museums (NCSM). It was established in honor of Bharat Ratna Sir M. Visvesvaraya, a renowned engineer and statesman.

The museum's mission is to promote scientific literacy and foster a sense of curiosity among visitors of all ages through interactive and engaging exhibits. It is spread across multiple floors, with each gallery dedicated to a different field of science and technology. Unlike traditional museums, VITM encourages visitors to touch, experiment, and learn through hands-on experiences, making it a popular destination for students and families.

Interactive Galleries

Engine Hall

A popular gallery featuring a range of engines, from vintage cars and locomotives to aviation engines and spacecraft models. The exhibits explain the principles of mechanical engineering and power generation in a simple, accessible way.

Space Gallery

This gallery takes visitors on an exciting journey through space exploration. It features detailed models of rockets, satellites, and planetary rovers, as well as displays on the history of space travel and India's contributions to space science.

Biotechnology Revolution

This modern gallery explores the fascinating world of biotechnology, with interactive exhibits on DNA, genetic engineering, and its applications in medicine and agriculture. It simplifies complex scientific concepts through engaging visuals and hands-on activities.

Electrotechnic Gallery

Learn about the principles of electricity and magnetism through a variety of fun experiments. The gallery features exhibits on basic circuits, power generation, and the history of electrical appliances, making it a great learning experience for all ages.
