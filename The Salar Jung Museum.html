Salar Jung Museum

Home to the world's largest one-man collection of art and artifacts.

About the Museum

The Salar Jung Museum, located in Hyderabad, is a testament to the passionate collecting of Nawab Mir Yousuf Ali Khan, also known as Salar Jung III. Established in 1951, it houses a magnificent collection of over 46,000 art objects, 9,000 manuscripts, and 60,000 printed books acquired from various parts of the world, making it one of the largest and most significant museums in India.

The museum's collection is unique because it was assembled by a single individual over a span of 40 years. After Salar Jung III's death, his family donated the entire collection to the nation. The museum was formally inaugurated by the first Prime Minister of India, Jawaharlal Nehru, and in 1961, was declared an "Institution of National Importance" by an Act of Parliament.

Highlights of Our Collections

The Musical Clock

One of the museum's most famous attractions is the British-made musical clock. Every hour, a miniature timekeeper figure emerges from the clock and strikes a gong to announce the time, captivating visitors with its intricate mechanical work and design.

Veiled Rebecca

A masterpiece of neo-classical sculpture, the "Veiled Rebecca" is a stunning marble statue created by Italian artist Giovanni Maria Benzoni. It showcases the artist's incredible skill in making the marble appear as a translucent veil draped over the subject's face, a major draw for art enthusiasts.

Indian Art and Decorative Objects

The museum's Indian collection includes a wide variety of miniature paintings, stone and bronze sculptures, textiles, and ivory carvings. It also features a magnificent collection of Bidriware, which is an ancient metal handicraft from Bidar, known for its intricate inlay work.

Far Eastern and European Art

The Salar Jung Museum's collections are truly global. The Far Eastern section displays artifacts from China and Japan, including porcelain, lacquerware, and embroidery. The European collection is equally impressive, with a vast range of oil paintings, furniture, and decorative arts from England, France, and Italy.
